## Participant Expectations

Participation in the Adobe Feedback Program is entirely voluntary and designed to fit a variety of interests, products, and levels of engagement. Participants may be invited to:

* Evaluate new features and test emerging technologies
* Share feedback through surveys, benchmark studies, and concept reviews
* Participate in research activities including usability testing
* Engage directly with Adobe product teams in strategy sessions

Opportunities vary based on factors such as product area, region, customer profile, and program availability, and not all participants will be invited to every opportunity. Your feedback helps Adobe improve products, prioritize future investments, and deliver better customer experiences.

## Frequently Asked Questions

+++ Who can participate?

Anyone, regardless of job level, is welcome to participate. We value feedback from individuals with all levels of experience and across all relevant job functions. There is no limit on the number of representatives a company may enroll in the Program. Please note, employee participation is at the sole discretion of the participating company.

+++

+++ Does participation cost anything?

There is no monetary cost to participate.

+++

+++ Can I leave the program at any time?

Yes. While we'd love to hear your feedback, we understand that circumstances can change. Participation is entirely voluntary, and you may opt out at any time if needed.

+++

+++ Are there confidentiality requirements?

Yes. Participants in the Program will receive Adobe confidential information and must not share or discuss it outside the Program.

+++
