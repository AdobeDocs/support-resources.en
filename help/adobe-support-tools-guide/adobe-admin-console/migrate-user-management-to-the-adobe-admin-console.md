# Migrate user management to the Adobe Admin Console

Applies to enterprise.

Learn how eligible enterprise accounts can migrate user management to the Adobe Admin Console.

The [Adobe Admin Console](https://adminconsole.adobe.com/) is the central place where administrators manage users, licenses, and product access across Adobe solutions.

Migration to Admin Console-based user management is available for eligible accounts. Eligibility varies by solution and may depend on factors such as:

- product version or platform generation
- organization configuration
- identity and domain setup
- contract or provisioning model
- product-specific migration readiness requirements

If your account is eligible, sign in to the [Adobe Admin Console](https://adminconsole.adobe.com/) and work with your Adobe account team to review the migration process and timeline. If your account is not yet eligible, contact your Adobe account team to understand the requirements for enablement.

## Before you begin

Before starting a migration, confirm the following:

- your organization has access to the [Adobe Admin Console](https://adminconsole.adobe.com/)
- your identity strategy is defined
- any required domains or directories are configured
- your solution supports migration to Admin Console-based user management
- any solution-specific prerequisites have been completed

## Solution-specific eligibility

Migration requirements are not the same for every Adobe solution.

Some solutions may require:

- a specific product version
- migration to a newer platform
- coordination with an Adobe account team
- limitations based on organization configuration
- staged rollout or enablement by Adobe

Refer to the documentation for your Adobe solution for exact eligibility and migration steps.

## Next steps

After eligibility is confirmed:

1. Sign in to the [Adobe Admin Console](https://adminconsole.adobe.com/).
1. Review your organization's identity and user-management setup.
1. Confirm the target solution supports Admin Console-based user management.
1. Work with your Adobe account team on migration timing and rollout.
1. Communicate any sign-in or access changes to affected administrators and users.
