# Enforce password expiration in the Admin Console

**Applies to:** Enterprise & Teams

Organization system admins can follow the process to set a password expiration time limit for their users. Sign in to the [Adobe Admin Console](https://adminconsole.adobe.com/).

## Steps

1. Go to the [Adobe Admin Console](https://adminconsole.adobe.com/settings) **[!UICONTROL Settings]** section and expand the **[!UICONTROL Privacy and security]** option on the upper-left.

1. Select **[!UICONTROL Authentication settings]**.

1. Review the **[!UICONTROL Authentication levels]** and select the most suitable level of authentication for your users: **[!UICONTROL Easiest for Users]**, **[!UICONTROL More Secure]**, **[!UICONTROL Most Secure]**.

Visit the [Authentication settings Helpx](https://helpx.adobe.com/enterprise/using/authentication-settings.html) to know more about authentication and security policies available.
